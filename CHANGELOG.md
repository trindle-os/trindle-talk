# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 26 October 2026

First release.

- Press Talk in the ring, speak, and your words appear under Talk's name in the ring.
- Takes out um and er, and puts American spellings into British ones, if you want.
- Listens in 19 languages, or works out which one you are speaking.
- Read back says your words aloud in English, in one of ten Supertonic voices.
- Listening and speaking happen on your own computer, using the Trindle Voice engine.
- The speaking pace is set to sound natural, and the speech plays at the right speed.
- Reads back through the speech service the system image installs when there is one, and takes voice files from /usr/share/trindle-voice first; otherwise it starts its own copy of the program.
